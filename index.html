Built so strangers can't see you

Privacy is enforced by the database's own rules, not just hidden on the page. Even someone poking at the API gets nothing they shouldn't.

Campus email only

Sign-in is a one-time link sent to your campus inbox. No passwords to leak, and the database rejects anyone without a campus address.

Contact details stay sealed

Your phone or handle is shown only to riders you approve. Everyone else on the board sees a locked field.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Every connection runs over HTTPS, and Supabase encrypts the stored database at rest.

Old rides disappear

Past trips drop off the board, and deleting a ride removes its requests and contact details with it.
